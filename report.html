1. Executive Summary

Global enterprises, diplomatic missions, and institutional leadership councils manage hundreds of formal occasion letters annually—spanning national holidays, head-of-state transitions, bilateral accords, and internal corporate milestones.

The Occasion Letters Workspace is a purpose-built desktop communication platform engineered to eliminate drafting friction, prevent severe diplomatic protocol blunders, guarantee multi-language rigor, and provide seamless, non-blocking collaborative review. By marrying an interactive, month-partitioned calendar grid with a context-anchored AI agent grounded in institutional handbooks, the platform transforms high-stakes drafting into a predictable, error-free workflow.

2. Key Problem Statements & Core Pain Points

Traditional organizational workflows for occasion letters suffer from severe systemic vulnerabilities:

Pain Point 1: Fragmented Observance Tracking
Occasions are tracked across disparate spreadsheets and personal calendars. Important national observances or state holidays are frequently identified too late, resulting in rushed, low-quality correspondence.
Solution: Unified Interactive Calendar Grid
A centralized Gantt-inspired calendar grid partitioned with a thin monthly schedule rail. Color-coded status washes provide instantaneous visibility into pending, drafting, and completed observances.
Pain Point 2: Protocol Errors & Honorific Mismatch
Diplomatic correspondence requires strict adherence to honorifics, titles, and jurisdictional customs. Generic AI drafting engines hallucinate informal phrasings that damage diplomatic standing.
Solution: Institutional Context Grounding
The AI drafting engine is strictly grounded in uploaded institutional PDF handbooks, past approved letter archives, and strict dual-track separation (External Diplomatic vs Internal Staff).
Pain Point 3: Translation & Typographic Friction
Managing multi-lingual dispatches (e.g., German, French, Arabic, Chinese) requires disjointed copy-pasting across translation tools, resulting in broken formatting and improper Right-to-Left (RTL) rendering.
Solution: Integrated Multi-Language Workbench
Native multi-tab language switching within the primary editor card with automated RTL bidirectional typesetting support and one-click AI translation actions.
Pain Point 4: Context Loss in Disjointed Review Cycles
Full-page redirects or modal popups sever the drafter’s spatial orientation within the monthly schedule, causing confusion during high-volume correspondence sprints.
Solution: Floating Non-Blocking Drawer Overlay
A refined right-docked floating panel allows full drafting, reviewing, provenance tracking, and AI chat without leaving or obscuring the primary calendar workspace.

4. End-to-End User Workflow

Phase A: Discovery & Ingestion

  • Automatic Ingestion: National holidays and diplomatic events sync automatically via Google Calendar or live Google Sheets.
  • Triage: Communications team views the upcoming month schedule in the calendar matrix, filtered by track or status.

Phase B: AI-Assisted Drafting & Provenance

  • Template Generation: Opening an event auto-generates a draft aligned with the occasion’s history (e.g., Swiss National Day).
  • Dual-Track Adaptation: Drafter switches between External (Formal Protocol) and Internal (Staff Notice) to produce tailored texts.
  • AI Refinement: One-click prompts polish diplomatic tone, adjust length, or translate to partner languages with full RTL text alignment.
  • Provenance Audit: Every draft displays historical base text and diff comparisons with past approved letters.

Phase C: Collaborative Review & Dispatch

  • Reviewer Hand-off: Drafter transitions status to In Review, notifying designated reviewers in the Notifications Stream.
  • Verification: Reviewer inspects language tabs, reviews target audience rosters, and clicks Approve or Request Changes.
  • Dispatch & Export: Approved letters are exported to verified distribution rosters or downloaded as official PDF/DOCX memos.
