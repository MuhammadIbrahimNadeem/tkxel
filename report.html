Executive Summary

Occasion Letters is an all-in-one workspace designed to help communications teams, executive offices, and diplomatic missions prepare and review official occasion letters (such as National Days, partner milestones, and company-wide announcements).

Instead of juggling scattered spreadsheets, email drafts, and manual translation tools, this platform brings everything into one unified, interactive space.

Main Problems & How We Solve Them

1. Missed Holidays & Disorganized Calendars
Problem: Important national days and partner observances are tracked in messy spreadsheets, leading to last-minute rushes.
Solution: A clear, interactive monthly calendar that highlights upcoming events, their drafting status, and required languages at a glance.
2. Inconsistent Tone & Protocol Mistakes
Problem: Letters to ambassadors and partners need strict diplomatic wording, while internal staff notes need a warmer tone.
Solution: Built-in dual-track drafting (External vs Internal) powered by an AI assistant that references approved institutional guidelines and past letters.
3. Slow Multi-Language Translations
Problem: Copying text across external translation tools breaks formatting and mishandles languages like Arabic.
Solution: One-click translation tabs directly inside the editor, with native Right-to-Left (RTL) typography support.
4. Slow, Messy Review Cycles
Problem: Drafts get lost in long email chains, and feedback is hard to track.
Solution: A floating right-side panel where drafters write and reviewers can review, leave feedback, and approve with a single click.

Core Product Features

Feature What It Does
Interactive Calendar Shows all monthly events with clean color blocks, status tags, and language count badges.
Floating Draft Drawer Opens from the right without covering your calendar, letting you draft and edit seamlessly.
AI Comms Assistant Helps adjust tone (formal vs warm), translate languages, and generate quick suggestions.
Workspace Connectors Connects to Google Calendar, Google Sheets, policy PDF files, and recipient email lists.
Notification Feed A clean, full-width list that alerts reviewers when a letter is ready for approval.

How It Works (Simple 3 Steps)

  1. Ingest & Schedule: The calendar automatically syncs upcoming observances from your connected Google Calendar or spreadsheets.
  2. Draft & Refine: Click any event to open the draft drawer. Use AI prompts to fine-tune the wording or translate into other languages.
  3. Review & Send: Switch to Reviewer mode to inspect the draft, view recipient rosters, and click Approve to finish.
